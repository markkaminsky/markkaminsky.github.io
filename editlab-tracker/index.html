EditLab Tracker

A fully featured music tracker for the Mac, with a song splitter.

EditLab Tracker uses a vertical tracker interface to sequence samples and notes: the song runs down the screen one row at a time, and you play notes straight from your keyboard. Built-in synth sounds, your own audio, a mixer with real meters, effect racks and automation are all in one window.

It also has a song splitter. Drop a finished song onto the window and it comes back as a kit of its own sounds: kick, snare, hat, bass and the rest, named where the app can tell them apart. Play them, rearrange them, and remix the song or build something new from it.

What it does

  • Pattern grid with note, instrument, volume, pan and effect columns; patterns arranged in an order list
  • Sample and synth instruments, your own audio in any slot, a sample editor with cut, copy, paste and loop points
  • A mixer with a fader, pan, mute, solo and a live meter per channel, and a master bus
  • Effect racks on every channel and the master, with seventeen built-in effects, plus effect designs from .eltrkfx files
  • Automation of volume, pan, effect parameters and tempo
  • A song splitter that turns a finished MP3, WAV or M4A into a playable kit
  • Export as WAV or M4A; songs saved as .eltrk files
  • No account, no subscription, no data collected
