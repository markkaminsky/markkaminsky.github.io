EditLab Tracker

For the Mac · Version 1.8

Fix the one part of your AI song that went wrong. Keep the rest.

Drop in a finished song and it comes back as a project you can edit: the vocal, the drums, and the bass, guitar and piano each on a track of its own. Until you change something, it plays back just like the original.

Get it on the Mac App Store

Free to download. Pro is a one-time $24.99 USD, with no subscription.

What you can do with it

  • Fix the bar that went wrongSwap it for a clean pass from elsewhere in the song.
  • Tame the fizzThe buzzy grain on an AI vocal gets its own track.
  • Make an instrumentalMute the vocal. Or solo it for an a cappella.
  • Replace one partNew drums or bass under the same vocal.
  • Tidy the introCut stray sounds and start clean on the downbeat.
  • Make a sample packExport the song's sounds, with Pro.

Works with Claude

Link Tracker to the Claude app and ask in plain words: “take the drums out of the intro”, “make the chorus hit harder”, “balance the mix”. Each change lands in the grid as you watch, and Edit ▸ Undo takes any of them back.

The details

  • Free: three splits, editing, saving, the demo songs, and Claude.
  • Pro, a one-time $24.99 USD: unlimited splits, WAV and M4A export, and sample packs.
  • Also a full tracker: pattern grid, piano, sample editor, mixer, effects and automation.
  • Needs macOS 14 or later. The vocal and instruments on their own tracks need Apple silicon and macOS 15.
  • Private: it runs on your Mac. No account, and Tracker uploads nothing.
