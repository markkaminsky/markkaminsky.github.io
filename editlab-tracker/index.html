EditLab Tracker

Fix your AI song, part by part. A full music tracker for the Mac, with a song splitter.

Drop a finished song onto the window, an AI track or your own, and it comes back as a kit of its own sounds: kick, snare, hat, bass and the rest, named where the app can tell them apart, laid out as a playable project. Replace the bar that went wrong, tame the hiss on just the part that has it, and keep everything else. Or rearrange the sounds and build something new from them.

Underneath is a vertical tracker for sequencing samples and notes: the song runs down the screen one row at a time, and you play notes straight from your keyboard. Built-in synth sounds, your own audio, a mixer with real meters, effect racks and automation are all in one window.

Get EditLab Tracker on the Mac App Store. It's free to download, and Pro is a one-time $24.99 USD.

What it does

  • Pattern grid with note, instrument, volume, pan and effect columns; patterns arranged in an order list
  • Sample and synth instruments, your own audio in any slot, a sample editor with cut, copy, paste and loop points
  • A mixer with a fader, pan, mute, solo and a live meter per channel, and a master bus
  • Effect racks on every channel and the master, with seventeen built-in effects, plus effect designs from .eltrkfx files
  • Automation of volume, pan, effect parameters and tempo
  • A song splitter that turns a finished MP3, WAV or M4A into a playable kit
  • Export as WAV or M4A with Pro; songs saved as .eltrk files
  • No account, no subscription, no data collected

Free and Pro

  • Free: write, edit, play and save songs, all the demo songs, and three splits of your own songs
  • Pro, a one-time $24.99 USD: unlimited splitting, WAV and M4A export, sample export, and saving splits as sample packs

Requirements

A Mac with macOS 14 or later. Everything runs on your Mac: no account, no network requests, and nothing uploaded.
