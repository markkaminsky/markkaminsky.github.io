Support

InternetSearch — help for people using the app and for App Review

What InternetSearch needs

  • iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, or later models that support it)
  • iOS 26 or later
  • Apple Intelligence turned on in System Settings

Without Apple Intelligence, the app shows a designed availability screen instead of inventing a broken experience.

How to get help

Open a GitHub issue (preferred for 1.0): github.com/markkaminsky/internetsearch/issues

Include: device model, iOS version, whether Apple Intelligence is on, and what you searched. Do not paste API keys.

Common questions

  • Results won’t author / “unavailable” messages. Turn on Apple Intelligence, confirm the device is supported, and try again on Wi‑Fi after a reboot.
  • No live images / Verify live does nothing useful. That path needs a search-provider API key in Settings. Without a key, results still author on-device; live verification and live imagery stay off by design.
  • Go deeper / “Grounded live”. Tap Go deeper on a results page. Settled topics can ground on Wikipedia with no key. Product/news-style queries use your configured provider when a key is saved.
  • Airplane mode. Authored results still work offline. Live verification and live grounding need a network.
  • Crisis resources. Queries that look like personal crisis intent show help resources (including 988 in the US) instead of a normal results page.

Privacy
