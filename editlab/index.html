EditLab

The editor that takes prompts. A macOS video editor that runs on your Mac.

Tell EditLab what you’re making and it lays out the shots, casts the character, and stamps the transitions — as ordinary clips and keyframes you can retime, restyle or delete by hand. It also repairs generated footage instead of re-rolling it: erase, blur or patch a region, and hold a face steady across a shot, baked into the export.

What it does

  • Prompt-seeded projects, storyboard placeholders, format arcs
  • Region repair and face continuity that ship in the export
  • Character animation: gaits, fights, props, stop-motion or smooth
  • Narration in your Mac’s voices, and voice control of the edit
  • Grand transitions, camera lanes, scene and effect cards, a sound kit
  • No account, no subscription, no data collected
